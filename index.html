01 / Research

Research Focus

My research combines physical phenomena, signal acquisition and diagnostic reasoning. The main goal is to understand what current waveforms can reveal about the internal behaviour and degradation of automotive injectors.

Injector Diagnostics

Non-invasive diagnostic methods based on electrical current signals.

Mathematical Modelling

Electro-mechanical-hydraulic injector models in MATLAB/Simulink.

Signal Analysis

Time-domain and frequency-domain features related to injector operation.
