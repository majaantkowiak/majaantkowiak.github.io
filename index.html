Selected Projects

Multidomain Simulation Research Project

Injector Multidomain Simulation

A coupled MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape representation of an electromagnetic direct-injection fuel injector, developed to investigate how electrical excitation propagates through electromagnetic force, needle dynamics and hydraulic fuel flow. The model reproduces the characteristic Peak & Hold current response and supports parameter-sensitivity studies and validation against laboratory measurements.

01 Electrical and electromagnetic behaviour
02 Needle dynamics and mechanical response
03 Hydraulic flow and injected fuel mass
04 Validation and parameter sensitivity
Simulated injector current waveform · Peak & Hold control
