01 / SIGNAL DIAGNOSTICS Core Research

Signal-Based Injector Diagnostics

Development of non-invasive diagnostic methods based on electrical current waveforms of electromagnetic fuel injectors. The research focuses on identifying signal features associated with injector operation, technical condition and progressive degradation.

02 / MODELLING Research Method

Injector Modelling

Mathematical and multidomain modelling is used to describe relationships between the electrical, electromagnetic, mechanical and hydraulic behaviour of the injector. Analytical models support interpretation of current and voltage transients, while numerical simulation enables investigation of coupled physical phenomena.

03 / DEGRADATION & RELIABILITY Ongoing Research

Degradation & Reliability Research

Current research also focuses on describing injector degradation as a measurable change in technical condition. Electrical, electromagnetic, mechanical, hydraulic and thermal degradation mechanisms are analysed together with their observable diagnostic indicators. The long-term objective is to combine these effects into a quantitative indicator describing injector condition and reliability.

Selected Projects

Multidomain Simulation Research Project

Injector Multidomain Simulation

A coupled MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape representation of an electromagnetic direct-injection fuel injector, developed to investigate how electrical excitation propagates through electromagnetic force, needle dynamics and hydraulic fuel flow. The model reproduces the characteristic Peak & Hold current response and supports parameter-sensitivity studies and validation against laboratory measurements.

01 Electrical and electromagnetic behaviour
02 Needle dynamics and mechanical response
03 Hydraulic flow and injected fuel mass
04 Validation and parameter sensitivity
Simulated injector current waveform · Peak & Hold control

Diagnostic Software Research Software

Injector Diagnostic Software

A LabVIEW-based research application developed for automated analysis of electromagnetic injector current signals. The software identifies individual injection events, detects characteristic waveform phases and extracts time-domain and frequency-domain diagnostic features. Reference-based comparison and diagnostic maps support evaluation of injector behaviour, while dedicated reporting tools summarize results obtained from individual injections and complete measurement series.

01 Automatic injection and phase detection
02 Time-domain feature extraction
03 FFT and frequency-domain analysis
04 Reference comparison and diagnostic mapping
Time-domain analysis and automatic injector current feature extraction
