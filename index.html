01 / About

About Me

I am a researcher and engineer focused on automotive mechatronic systems, signal analysis and diagnostic methods. My work combines mathematical modelling, experimental measurements and software-based analysis to better understand the behaviour of modern fuel injection systems.

I am particularly interested in the relationship between electrical signals and the mechanical and hydraulic behaviour of electromagnetic fuel injectors.

02 / Research

Research Focus

My research combines physical modelling, electrical signal analysis and experimental diagnostics of automotive injection systems.

R / 01

Injector Diagnostics

Development of non-invasive diagnostic methods based on electrical current signals generated during injector operation.

R / 02

Mathematical Modelling

Non-linear electro-mechanical-hydraulic modelling of gasoline direct injection systems using MATLAB and Simulink.

R / 03

Signal Analysis

Time-domain and frequency-domain analysis for identifying characteristic features associated with injector behaviour and degradation.
