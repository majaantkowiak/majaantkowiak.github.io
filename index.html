01 / Research

Research Focus

My research combines physical phenomena, signal acquisition and diagnostic reasoning. The main goal is to understand what current waveforms can reveal about the internal behaviour and degradation of automotive injectors.

Injector Diagnostics

Non-invasive diagnostic methods based on electrical current signals.

Mathematical Modelling

Electro-mechanical-hydraulic injector models developed in MATLAB and Simulink.

Signal Analysis

Time-domain and frequency-domain features related to injector operation.

Research Project In Development

Injector Multidomain Simulation

A coupled electrical, mechanical, hydraulic and thermal simulation of an electromagnetic direct injector, developed to reproduce its dynamic behaviour and electrical response.

01 Peak & Hold current control
02 Position-dependent inductance and electromagnetic force
03 Needle dynamics, fuel force and injected mass
04 Validation against measured current waveforms

Injector Diagnostic Software

A LabVIEW-based research application for automatic analysis of injector current waveforms and extraction of diagnostic information from individual injections and complete measurement series.

01 Automatic injection and phase detection
02 Time-domain feature extraction and derivative analysis
03 FFT-based frequency analysis and spectral features
04 Reference comparison, fault maps and report export

Analytical Study

Analytical Model of Injector Current & Voltage

Analytical description of the electrical response of an electromagnetic injector represented as an equivalent RL circuit, including peak, hold and switch-off transients.

τ = L R
