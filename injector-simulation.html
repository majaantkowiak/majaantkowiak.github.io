Project 01 · Multidomain Simulation

Injector Multidomain Simulation

A coupled simulation of an electromagnetic direct-injection fuel injector developed in MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape. The model connects electrical, electromagnetic, mechanical, hydraulic and thermal phenomena within one simulation environment.
MATLAB / Simulink Simscape Multidomain Modelling
01 / OVERVIEW

One injector. Several physical domains.

The project was developed to reproduce the dynamic behaviour of an electromagnetic fuel injector rather than treating the coil as an isolated electrical load. The electrical response of the solenoid is coupled with the motion of the injector needle and the hydraulic behaviour of the fuel path. Changes occurring in one domain therefore influence the response observed in the others, including the current waveform used for diagnostic analysis.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Model architecture

01

Electrical

Solenoid current, coil resistance, inductance and Peak & Hold control.

02

Electromagnetic

Conversion of electrical excitation into electromagnetic force acting on the needle.

03

Mechanical

Needle motion, spring force, damping and forces associated with injector operation.

04

Hydraulic & Thermal

Fuel flow, pressure-dependent behaviour, injected mass and temperature-dependent coil resistance.

03 / COUPLING

From electrical input to fuel delivery

Electrical excitation voltage · current · inductance
Electromagnetic force solenoid field · actuator force
Needle motion lift · velocity · opening
Fuel flow hydraulic response · injected mass
04 / PURPOSE

What the simulation is used for

01

Current waveform reproduction

Reproduction of the characteristic injector current response including the Peak and Hold operating phases.

02

Parameter sensitivity

Investigation of how changes in electrical and physical injector parameters affect the resulting response.

03

Internal-state analysis

Observation of quantities that are difficult to access directly during normal injector operation, such as needle displacement.

04

Diagnostic development

Use of simulated responses to support research on relationships between injector condition and measurable electrical signals.

05 / VALIDATION

Simulation versus measurement

Laboratory signal

Measured current waveform

Current signals recorded during real injector operation provide the reference used to assess whether the simulation reproduces the characteristic stages of the electrical response.

Model output

Simulated response

The model output is compared with the measured waveform with particular attention to the current rise, Peak region, transition to Hold, Hold-level behaviour and switch-off response.
